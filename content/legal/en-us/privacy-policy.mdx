## Your Rights Under LGPD and GDPR

If you are a resident of Brazil or the EEA, you have specific rights under the **Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**. These rights, subject to applicable limitations, include:

- **Right to Access:** Request information about personal data we collect, use, and disclose
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
- **Right to Deletion / Anonymization:** Request deletion or anonymization of personal data under specific circumstances
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive a copy of personal data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to Information about Sharing:** Be informed about public and private entities with which we share your data
- **Right to Restriction of Processing:** Request limitations on data processing
- **Right to Object:** Object to specific processing activities
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent for data processing at any time, where consent is the legal basis

To exercise these rights, contact us at [contact@optimize.deals](mailto:contact@optimize.deals). We will respond in accordance with legal requirements and the timeframes set by the LGPD or GDPR.
